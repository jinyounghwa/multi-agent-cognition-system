## How to Use This Guide

When you're doing your daily MCS reflection and feel stuck, use these prompts to jumpstart your thinking. Pick 2-3 questions from each perspective that feel most relevant to what you're learning.

**Don't answer all questions!** That would take too long. Choose the ones that spark curiosity.

---

## 💻 Developer/Technical Perspective

### Understanding How It Works
- How does this actually work under the hood?
- What are the key implementation details I should know?
- What algorithms or data structures are involved?
- What are the performance characteristics? (Time/space complexity)

### Technical Trade-offs
- What trade-offs does this technology make?
- What does it optimize for? What does it sacrifice?
- When would this approach be inefficient?
- What are the edge cases or limitations?

### Problem-Solving
- What problem does this technology solve?
- How did people solve this before this technology existed?
- What makes this solution better/worse than alternatives?
- Could I implement a simplified version myself?

### Technical Depth
- What are the dependencies or prerequisites?
- How does this integrate with other systems?
- What are common bugs or gotchas?
- What would I need to debug if this fails?

---

## 🏗️ Architect/Systems Perspective

### Real-World Application
- When would I actually use this in a production system?
- What happens if I don't use this? What breaks or becomes harder?
- At what scale does this become necessary?
- Is this solving a problem I actually have?

### System Design
- How does this fit into a larger architecture?
- What are the integration points?
- How does this affect system scalability?
- What's the operational overhead of using this?

### Long-Term Thinking
- Is this technology stable and mature?
- What's the learning curve for my team?
- How hard is this to maintain over time?
- What's the migration/upgrade path?

### Decision Making
- Should I use this for my current project?
- What would I use this for specifically?
- What are the simpler alternatives?
- What's the minimum version I could start with?

## 💰 Business/Revenue Perspective

### Value Creation
- How can this create value for customers?
- What customer pain point does this address?
- Who would pay for this knowledge or capability?
- Can I build a product/service around this?

### Market Opportunity
- Is there a market for this?
- How big is the potential market?
- Who are the current players?
- What's the competitive advantage?

### Monetization
- How could I charge for this?
- What's the pricing model? (One-time, subscription, usage-based)
- What's the customer acquisition cost?
- What's the potential profit margin?

### Business Validation
- Would customers actually pay for this?
- How can I validate this hypothesis quickly?
- What's the minimum viable product?
- Who can I talk to validate demand?

---

## 🌍 Market/Global Perspective

### Market Context
- Is this a global trend or local phenomenon?
- What's the adoption rate in different regions?
- Are there cultural differences in how this is used?
- Which markets are leading vs lagging?

### Competitive Landscape
- Who are the major players?
- What are they doing well/poorly?
- Where are the gaps in the market?
- Is this a crowded or blue ocean market?

### Industry Trends
- Is this technology growing or declining?
- What's the next evolution of this space?
- What are the adjacent technologies?
- What macro trends affect this?

### Global vs Local
- Is this problem universal or specific to certain markets?
- What local variations exist?
- Could this solution work in emerging markets?
- What adaptations would be needed?

---

## 📚 Learning/Education Perspective

### Understanding Check
- Can I explain this clearly to someone else?
- Can I explain it to a non-technical person?
- Can I draw a diagram to illustrate the concept?
- Can I teach this to a junior developer?

### Knowledge Gaps
- What parts do I still not understand?
- Where did I get confused?
- What assumptions did I make?
- What questions do I still have?

### Learning Efficiency
- How did I learn this? Was it effective?
- What resources were most helpful?
- What would I do differently next time?
- How can I remember this better?

### Teaching Others
- How would I structure a tutorial on this?
- What are the prerequisite concepts?
- What examples would make this clear?
- What are common misconceptions?

---

## 🧠 Philosophy/Cognitive Perspective

### Fundamental Principles
- What's the core principle at play here?
- Why does this work the way it does?
- What's the underlying abstraction?
- What's the "essence" of this concept?

### Pattern Recognition
- Have I seen this pattern before in other domains?
- What other fields use similar concepts?
- What's the meta-pattern?
- Is this a specific instance of a general principle?

### Transferable Knowledge
- Where else can I apply this principle?
- How does this relate to other things I know?
- What universal truth does this reveal?
- How does this change my mental models?

### Deep Questions
- Why is this important in the grand scheme?
- What does this teach me about how systems work?
- How does this relate to human psychology/behavior?
- What philosophical implications does this have?

---

## 🎯 Bonus: Meta-Cognitive Questions

### About Your Thinking Process
- Which perspective came most naturally today?
- Which perspective was hardest? Why?
- Did I discover any unexpected connections?
- How is my multi-perspective thinking improving?

### About MCS Itself
- How long did this reflection take?
- Did I feel rushed or did I go too deep?
- What made this reflection valuable (or not)?
- How can I improve tomorrow's reflection?

---

## Tips for Using These Prompts

### 1. **Don't Be Rigid**
You don't need to answer questions in order. Jump around. Follow curiosity.

### 2. **Quality Over Quantity**
Better to answer 2-3 questions deeply than 10 questions superficially.

### 3. **Customize for Context**
Modify these questions to fit what you're learning. Make them specific.

### 4. **Save Your Favorites**
Some questions will resonate more than others. Create your own "greatest hits" list.

### 5. **Notice Patterns**
Over time, you'll develop your own questioning style. That's the goal!

## Example: Applying Prompts to PostgreSQL Indexing

### What you're learning:
"PostgreSQL B-tree indexes"

### Developer perspective prompt chosen:
"What trade-offs does this technology make?"

### Your answer:
"B-tree indexes trade disk space and write performance for dramatically faster read performance. Specifically..."

### Why this worked:
The trade-off question led you to discover the core space-time tradeoff, which then connected to fundamental CS principles in the Philosophy perspective.

## Remember

**The goal of these prompts is to:**
- ✅ Trigger deep thinking
- ✅ Overcome mental blocks
- ✅ Ensure you're thinking from truly different angles

**Not to:**
- ❌ Create busywork
- ❌ Make reflection mechanical
- ❌ Replace genuine curiosity

**Use prompts as training wheels. Eventually, you'll internalize these questions and won't need the list.**
