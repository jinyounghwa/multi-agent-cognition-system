## Philosophy on Tools | 도구에 대한 철학 | ツールの哲学

**Tools should enhance thinking, not replace it.**

- ✅ Use tools to reduce friction
- ✅ Automate repetitive parts
- ✅ Focus your energy on deep analysis

- ❌ Don't let tools make you lazy
- ❌ Don't automate the thinking itself
- ❌ Don't lose the human insight

**The goal:** Spend less time on logistics, more time on insight generation.
